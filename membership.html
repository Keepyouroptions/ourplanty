Our Planty Membership

Grow your standing the same way you grow your plants.

Every member starts as a Dirt Digger. From there, your rank rises with what you actually do on Our Planty — sales completed and growers you help along the way. No tier is for sale.

$15

one-time membership fee to unlock your Our Planty shop and start listing plants for sale.

The Membership Ladder

Rank is earned, not bought.

Rank is earned through lifetime sales activity, not how much you pay. Every grower can reach Bloom Royalty.

Tier 1

Dirt Digger

Join with the one-time $15 fee

  • List and sell on Our Planty
  • Access to grower guides and resources
Tier 2

Petal Pusher

5 completed sales or $150 in lifetime sales

  • Everything in Dirt Digger
  • Featured in "New & Notable Growers"
  • Reduced marketplace fee
Tier 3

Garden Giggler

20 completed sales or $600 in lifetime sales

  • Everything in Petal Pusher
  • Priority placement in search and browse
  • Invited to submit a Grower Spotlight story
Tier 4

Bloom Royalty

60 completed sales or $2,000 in lifetime sales, plus mentoring one newer grower

  • Everything in Garden Giggler
  • Lowest marketplace fee on Our Planty
  • Invited to co-host a workshop or Q&A
  • Bloom Royalty badge on every listing

Your rank is yours for good. Once you reach a tier, you keep it. We also mark your shop Active if you've completed a sale in the past 12 months, so buyers can see who's currently growing and selling — a quiet season won't cost you your rank.

Start as a Dirt Digger. See where it grows.

Your $15 join fee and every sale you make support Our Planty and Mountain and Sea Foundation's mission to teach the joy of gardening.

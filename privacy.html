Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 19, 2026. This page explains what information Our Planty collects, why, and how it's handled.

Who we are

Our Planty (ourplanty.com) is a community gardening initiative operated by the Mountain and Sea Foundation, a Delaware-incorporated 501(c)(3) private operating foundation based in Chattanooga, Tennessee. This policy covers ourplanty.com. If you have questions about this policy or your information, contact us at hello@ourplanty.com.

What information we collect

Our Planty's website is a static site with no user accounts, no database, and no server-side processing. We do not use cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels on this site.

The only information we collect is what you choose to give us through our contact form or by emailing us directly — typically your name, email address, and any message content you provide, including which membership tier you're interested in if you select one. Submitting the contact form opens your own email application with that information pre-filled; it is sent from your device directly to hello@ourplanty.com and is not stored on our servers, because we don't have any.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions, comments, or membership interest.
  • To communicate with you about Our Planty and Mountain and Sea Foundation programs, if you've reached out to us.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Children's privacy

Our Planty's website is intended for adult gardeners and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this site.

Your choices

You can always reach out to hello@ourplanty.com to ask what information we hold about you, request that we delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you.

Changes to this policy

As Our Planty grows — including our planned marketplace platform launching in 2027 — this policy will be updated to reflect any new data we collect, such as account information or transaction history, and we'll update the "last updated" date above when that happens.

Our Story

Personal growth fuels collective good.

Our Planty is a vibrant online community for all gardeners, dedicated to helping you Unlock the Power of Your Garden. As a proud initiative of the Mountain and Sea Foundation, we provide a unique ecosystem where personal growth fuels collective good. Our members have the opportunity to sell their homegrown plants through Our Planty, with a portion of sales proceeds donated directly to the foundation to support our shared mission: promoting the joy of gardening and sharing the love of nature.

Mission & Vision

Mission Statement

  • Empower a community of gardeners to sell their plants through Our Planty.
  • Generate support to teach children about plants, arts, and science.
  • Promote the joy of gardening.
  • Share the love of nature.

Vision Statement

A world where every garden is a classroom and a source of philanthropic growth, ensuring the next generation is rooted in environmental stewardship and scientific curiosity.
